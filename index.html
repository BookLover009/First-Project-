How to Choose the Right Book for You

"A reader lives a thousand lives before he dies. The man who never reads lives only once." ~ George R. R. Martin

  • Pick a genre that you're in the mood for
  • Come up with tropes you do or not want to see in your book
  • Follow the next steps!
  1. See if any books on your physical tbr or virtual one fit your criteria
  2. Ask friends and check book communities online for recomendation
  3. Check out some of my recs down below!
  4. Once you've picked your book, go on to the next step!
  5. Honorable mention: manga and listening to audiobooks! They count and they're amazing options!

The Perfect Environment

    The Add Ons

  • If you're into annotating on a physical book, I reccomned trying colored tabs, clear sticky notes, mildliners, and pen or pencil to have fun!
  • Try matching the colors to those of your book!
  • If you're reading on a kindle, maybe try a clicker or stand.
  • If you're, reading in the dark, try out a customizable book light.

    • The Drinks

  • If you're reading in the morning or on the go, coffee of your choice always goes well.
  • If you like tea, juice, or match, have it!
  • Have fun with your cups and flavors!
  • Don't forget to sip on water to stay hydrated!

    • The Setting of Your Story

  • If you plan on getting into your book, don't forget to get some snacks to fuel you!
  • For a perfect day, a cozy and warm outfit, blanket, and spot in a bed or couch is a must!
  • Set the lighting, get your reading or favorite playlist on, get comfortable, and start your adventure!
