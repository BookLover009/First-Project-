How to Choose the Right Book for You

  • Pick a genre that you're in the mood for
  • Come up with tropes you do or not want to see in your book
  • Follow the next steps!
  1. See if any books on your physical tbr or virtual one fit your criteria
  2. Ask friends and check book communities online for recomendation
  3. Check out some of my recs down below!
  4. Once you've picked your book, get reading! Get your drink, get in your favorite spot, get annotation supplies if you want, and start your book!
